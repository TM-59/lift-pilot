FIELD NOTES

Height is energy in reserve.

Raise the nose and speed can become height. Go beyond the stall angle and lift falls while drag rises. The speed–height experiment adds 10% airspeed to a trimmed glide; the measured trace shows the resulting phugoid.

A deliberately small model

Point-mass motion, RK4 at 0.02 s, constant air density and a parabolic drag polar. No wind, thermals, roll or pitch-rate dynamics. Flight simulation for exploration; not flight instruction.
